# Design derivation and coordinated changes

Use when the request involves deriving, calculating, selecting or allocating a
design result, or carrying a change across related artifacts. These instructions
do not require a new register, schema, database or fixed authoring sequence.

## Establish the basis needed for the change

Find the applicable input items, revisions, units, constraints, design criteria
and existing decisions. Distinguish supplied facts, derived results, approved
provisional choices and unknowns. Identify authority at the fact or decision
level; one workbook may contain both source requirements and derived allocations.
Do not choose authority from format, filename, recency or convenient agreement.

Use existing IDs and references to connect source items, derivation and affected
outputs. A copied value without its applicability or units may change meaning.
Resolve duplicate or ambiguous identities before assigning the affected result.
Ask only for a missing decision that materially changes that result, after
checking the available basis; continue independent authorized work.

## Derive, select and allocate

Apply the project's actual formulas, capacity definitions, grouping rules and
constraints. Show the reasoning at the level needed to review the result in the
existing calculation, document or response. Never import limits or spare rules
from a convenient historical example as current requirements.

Where needed, check dimensional consistency, aggregation scope, rounding, units,
constraints and allocation coverage. A numerical result does not itself select
a product, approve a design or establish compliance. Preserve qualified or
conditional conclusions when a required input remains unresolved.

## Propagate changes and feedback

Follow the affected relationships into authorized documents, lists and drawings.
Carry additions, removals and changed identifiers as well as quantities. Update
cross-references and revision descriptions under existing project conventions.

A drawing, analysis or test may reveal a constraint requiring a changed input or
decision. Report that constraint against its actual source, resolve the decision
through the project's existing authority, and update the relevant authorized
source/candidate and dependent outputs. Do not make unrestricted bidirectional
sync, silently modify protected originals, or change inputs merely to make outputs
agree. Identify any necessary out-of-scope update and its impact on completion.

## Check the result

Check the affected derivation from its input basis and the result's transcription
into each affected output. Several matching outputs may share the same error.
For independent review, make the actual inputs, criteria and candidate available;
the author's account or self-check is not a replacement for that evidence.

Choose additional checks for a specific dependency, uncertainty or failure.
Unrelated calculations and whole-project tests are not a default consequence of
one document edit. State what is complete, conditionally supported or unverified
within the requested stage, without adding an unrequested tracking system.
